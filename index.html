ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Preparing Kenyans to Benefit from the 2027 Campaign Economy

The Clover Africa Initiative is a national economic empowerment programme designed to prepare young people, women, entrepreneurs, MSMEs and communities to participate ethically and profitably in the opportunities created by the 2027 campaign economy.

Through practical training, mentorship, financial literacy, business development and strategic partnerships, the initiative equips participants with the knowledge, confidence and networks required to build sustainable businesses beyond the election period.

TRAINING

How The Programme Will Be Conducted

Every participant follows a structured journey designed to transform knowledge into practical opportunities and long-term success.

1

Registration

Interested participants register for the programme and receive onboarding information.

2

Orientation

Introduction to the campaign economy, programme objectives and expectations.

3

Training Sessions

Practical learning facilitated by industry experts and experienced trainers.

4

Mentorship

Personalized guidance and coaching to help participants implement their ideas.

5

Business Opportunities

Participants connect with networks, partnerships and emerging opportunities.

6

Graduation

Participants complete the programme and continue receiving post-training support.

OUR TRAINING PROGRAMME

Building Skills for the Campaign Economy

Our practical training programme equips participants with the knowledge, tools and confidence needed to identify, access and sustain opportunities created within the campaign economy.

Campaign Economy

Understanding economic opportunities created before, during and after elections.

Business Development

Learn how to position your business to secure contracts and long-term growth.

Political Branding

Branding, marketing and communication strategies for campaign-related businesses.

Financial Literacy

Budgeting, pricing, costing and managing business finances effectively.

Networking

Build partnerships and relationships that unlock new opportunities.

Innovation

Develop creative solutions and sustainable business ideas for future growth.
